# Pisen: Competitive Research and Product Collateral Production

> This case was organized from customer-provided materials; the quantitative results were not independently audited by this project. Use it together with your own organization's data, permissions, and human-review requirements. V2.0 keeps the text readable but does not auto-promote customer statements into verified public cases.

## Conclusions First: From Problem to Reusable Artifacts

### Competitive Research

#### The Problem

A dozen-plus product managers need to continuously browse competitor pages and track new products and market moves. Information is scattered and fields inconsistent; a single analysis report takes most of a day from search to organization, and key changes are easily missed.

#### The Final Artifacts

A competitor analysis report with source links, change summaries, competitive judgments, and items awaiting verification, plus a set of research fields and verification checklists the team can reuse.

#### If You Want to Reuse

1. Replace the competitor list, public sources, collection frequency, and report fields with your own data and rules.
2. Start with a short time window, generating information cards and report drafts by new products, prices, selling points, channel moves, and evidence links.
3. Against the original pages, mark conflicting information, inferences, and items awaiting verification.
4. Have product managers re-check sources, add business judgment, and confirm conclusions.

Must be replaced with your own data, rules, or materials: the competitor list, public sources, collection frequency, report fields, and historical baseline cannot be carried over directly.

Results from customer material cannot be transplanted directly; retest with your own baseline hours, information counts, and verification time. The human confirmer is the product manager; external judgments also need confirmation from the corresponding business owner.

#### Evidence Boundaries

Customer materials state that competitive research went from about 5–6 hours per person per day to about 1 hour, with the saved time invested in product innovation. This is a customer statement, not independently audited by this project, and does not represent the results other organizations will obtain.

### Product Collateral Production

#### The Problem

Product managers need to produce product display images for suppliers and customers. Previously this required queueing with the design department, and ToB communication images were usually lower priority than e-commerce hero images, with delivery cycles of about half a month.

#### The Final Artifacts

A product display image draft ready for human review, plus a set of reusable input templates and a brand review checklist for the team.

#### If You Want to Reuse

1. Replace product parameters, appearance images, target users, layout dimensions, and brand guidelines with your own materials and rules.
2. Generate low-risk collateral drafts from fixed templates; do not publish directly.
3. Have product managers verify parameters, selling points, and use scenarios.
4. Have the design or brand owner confirm image authorization, brand expression, and the final release version.

Must be replaced with your own data, rules, or materials: product parameters, appearance images, target users, layout dimensions, and brand guidelines all need re-confirmation.

Results from customer material cannot be transplanted directly; build a baseline from your own queue wait times, draft generation, review, and rework records. The human confirmers are the product manager and the design or brand owner.

#### Evidence Boundaries

Customer materials state that collateral production went from about half a month to about half an hour, reducing design queue waits. This is a customer statement, not independently audited by this project, and does not represent the results other organizations will obtain.

## The Two Scenarios at a Glance

| Scenario | Old way | AI workflow | Customer-stated result |
|------|----------|-----------|--------------|
| Competitive research | Multiple product managers browsing pages separately, tracking moves, organizing manually | Fixed research fields, periodic public information collection, structured analysis report generation | From about 5–6 hours per person per day to about 1 hour |
| Product collateral production | Product team queueing with design for supplier and customer communication images | Input product parameters and appearance images; generate uniformly styled collateral drafts | From about half a month to about half an hour |

## Scenario and Problem

Guangdong Pisen Electronics Co., Ltd. serves the consumer electronics and digital accessories market, selling to multiple regions. The case focuses on two high-frequency tasks: organizing competitor moves, and producing product display materials for suppliers and customers. The old way relied on many people manually browsing, summarizing, and coordinating with design scheduling—long cycles and unstable output formats.

## Applicable Roles

Product managers, product marketing, market research, sales support, and design collaborators. Suited to consumer electronics or hardware teams that need continuous competitor tracking and fast production of standardized product materials.

## Input Materials

### Competitive Research

- Publicly accessible competitor pages, industry news, and product links.
- Competitor list, collection frequency, analysis fields, and historical reports.

### Product Collateral Production

- Your own product parameters, appearance images, target audiences, and use scenarios.
- Confirmed brand guidelines, material dimensions, and review requirements.

## Capabilities Used

Public information retrieval and organization, structured report generation, image-and-text collateral draft generation, and packaging fixed requirements into repeatable workflows. Specific available capabilities are governed by the official Qwen Work product documentation.

## Task Description

### Competitive Research

Turn "track public competitor information daily and output analysis" into a structured task with a fixed cadence, unified fields, and retained sources.

### Product Collateral Production

Turn "produce product display images for supplier and customer communication" into a standardized delivery that takes product parameters and appearance images as input and is published after human review.

## Execution Steps

### Competitive Research

1. Define the competitor scope, collection frequency, and report fields.
2. Generate information cards and structured reports by new products, prices, selling points, channel moves, and evidence links.
3. Product managers verify facts, add judgment, and confirm conclusions.

### Product Collateral Production

1. Build an input list of parameters, appearance images, target users, dimensions, brand guidelines, and forbidden expressions.
2. Generate low-risk drafts.
3. Product, design, or brand owners confirm copy, images, and external commitments.

In both scenarios, the confirmed prompts, fields, and review rules can be distilled into team templates, with periodic reviews of false positives, omissions, and rework causes.

## Final Artifacts

### Competitive Research

- A competitor analysis brief with source links and items awaiting verification.
- Research fields and verification checklists the team can reuse.

### Product Collateral Production

- Product display material drafts for suppliers or customers.
- Input templates and a brand review checklist the team can reuse.

## Acceptance Criteria

### Competitive Research

- Every external fact traces to a public source; inferences and facts labeled separately.
- Fixed report structure; product managers can review and decide within limited time.

### Product Collateral Production

- Product parameters, brand expression, and image usage all confirmed by owners.
- Output contains no internal costs, unreleased plans, personal information, or unauthorized assets.

## Permission and Security Boundaries

Use only information permitted for public retrieval and authorized product materials. Where unreleased products, supply-chain prices, customer lists, internal design source files, or personal data are involved, process them in a controlled space and obtain the corresponding owner's approval before external use.

## Reproducible Evidence

### Competitive Research

Customer materials state competitive research went from manual work by many people at about 5–6 hours per person per day to about 1 hour. When reproducing, record baseline hours per report, information counts, verification time, and conclusion adoption rate.

### Product Collateral Production

Customer materials state product collateral delivery went from about half a month to about half an hour. When reproducing, record queue wait time, draft generation time, human review time, rework counts, and final adoption rate.

All of the above are customer statements and do not represent the results other organizations will obtain.
