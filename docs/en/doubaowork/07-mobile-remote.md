# Remote Control Your Computer from Your Phone: Stay Connected on the Go

Some tasks run for ten-plus minutes, and you can't exactly sit there holding your laptop. Doubao Work's "remote control from phone" fits the scenario where **you've stepped away from your computer but want the task to keep running**: check progress from outside, add one more instruction, and the machine actually doing the work is still your computer—step out for a bit, and you can direct your desktop from the back of a taxi.

## Setup

1. Sign in to the **same Doubao account** on your phone and computer;
2. On the computer, open "Remote Control from Phone" and enable "Allow control by devices signed in to the same account";
3. For long-running jobs, also turn on "**Keep this device awake**"—your task won't be interrupted by a lock screen while you do other things; remember to turn it off when done;
4. In the Doubao mobile app, open a work task and select the online computer.

For your first connection, send a **read-only test** to confirm your phone is paired with the right computer:

```text
Tell me the name of the most recent file in the Downloads folder.
```

## Good Scenarios for This

- Checking the progress of a long task already running on your computer;
- Having Doubao find candidate files in a specified folder and return a summary;
- Continuing to organize local materials, spreadsheets, or project files;
- Giving the next instruction from your phone when a task is waiting for confirmation;
- Receiving progress notifications, then returning to the computer to check the final output when needed.

## A Real Moment

You suddenly need to revise a summary, but you're away from your desk: open Doubao Work on your phone, connect to your own computer, have Doubao locate that summary on the machine first, then make the edits, and send it straight back to a colleague via Feishu—without touching the computer once, you watch it get done from your phone.
