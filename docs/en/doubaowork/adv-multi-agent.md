# Multi-Agent (Work Squad) in Practice

When a task involves **multiple stages—research, analysis, writing, and review**—multi-agent (called "Work Squad" in Doubao Work) genuinely saves time—provided every role knows what it's doing, what it delivers, and to whom.

## Who's on the Squad

Take the "Hit Content Creation Squad": open it, click "Chat," and you'll see four members—**Creative Director (lead), Hit Marketing Planner, Hit Content Specialist, and AI Illustrator**. The lead's job is mainly creative direction, dividing the work, and the final wrap-up delivery.

## Just Say It Plainly—It Divides the Work Itself

Simply state your need in the chat box, for example:

> I run a cattery in my neighborhood with a dozen-plus cats. I want to post on Douyin but have no idea what to film. Help me figure out what to shoot in the first month.

Doubao Work will assign an appropriate set of expert agents to follow the task, and the experts collaborate to finish it—you don't need to write the division of labor for it (though for complex tasks, it's still best to confirm the split before work begins, as in the [previous chapter](/en/doubaowork/adv-buddy-or-squad)).

## An Entry Point That's Easy to Lose

After you send a task, if you navigate away to other screens and need to find it again: look under **"Buddy Chats → Tasks"**, not in your recent regular work task list.

## When Splitting into a Squad Is Worth It

| Signal | Explanation |
| --- | --- |
| Three or more stages with distinct roles | Planning, writing, design, and review each carry their own professional standards |
| Hand-off dependencies exist | The illustrator waits on the copy throughline; review waits on all deliverables |
| You're single-handedly covering several roles | The clearest sign it's time to outsource to a squad |

Conversely, for one-off tasks within a single professional scope (analyzing one spreadsheet, revising one draft), starting a new task or finding a single Work Buddy is faster and saves tokens.
