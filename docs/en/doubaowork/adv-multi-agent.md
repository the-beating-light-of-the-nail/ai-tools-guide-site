# How Do You Run a Multi-Agent Squad? Say It Plainly and It Splits the Work Itself

You've got a job in hand that needs research, analysis, drafting, and a final review—several stages chained together, and doing it alone is a headache. This is where multi-agent (called "Work Squad" in Doubao Work) genuinely saves time—but only if every role knows what it's doing, what it hands over, and to whom. This page covers how to get a squad running smoothly, plus that entry point people can never find.

## Who's on the Squad

Open the "Hit Content Creation Squad" and click "Chat," and you'll see four members—**Creative Director (lead), Hit Marketing Planner, Hit Content Specialist, and AI Illustrator**. The lead handles creative direction, dividing up the work, and the final wrap-up delivery.

## Just Say It Plainly—It Divides the Work Itself

Simply state your need in the chat box, for example:

> I run a cattery in my neighborhood with a dozen-plus cats. I want to post on Douyin but have no idea what to film. Help me figure out what to shoot in the first month.

Doubao Work will assign an appropriate set of expert agents to follow the task, and the experts collaborate to finish it—you don't need to write the division of labor for it (though for complex tasks, it's still best to confirm the split before work begins, as in the [previous chapter](/en/doubaowork/adv-buddy-or-squad)).

## An Entry Point That's Easy to Lose

After you send a task, if you navigate away to other screens and need to find it again: look under **"Buddy Chats → Tasks"**, not in your recent regular work task list. Remember this one and you won't lose track of where a job got to.

## When Splitting into a Squad Is Worth It

Not sure whether to call a squad? Check the signals against this table:

| Signal | Explanation |
| --- | --- |
| Three or more stages with distinct roles | Planning, writing, design, and review each carry their own professional standards |
| Hand-off dependencies exist | The illustrator waits on the copy throughline; review waits on all deliverables |
| You're single-handedly covering several roles | The clearest sign it's time to outsource to a squad |

Conversely, for one-off tasks within a single professional scope (analyzing one spreadsheet, revising one draft), starting a new task or finding a single Work Buddy is faster and saves tokens. There's no need to assemble a squad just for show.

## FAQ

**Can I just walk away and let the squad finish on its own?**
You only need to state the requirement clearly in the chat box and it divides the work up itself. For complex jobs, though, have it list the split first and confirm before it starts—otherwise the direction drifts and you redo everything.

**Where did the task I sent go?**
Look under "Buddy Chats → Tasks," not in your regular work task list. It's an entry point only squads have, and the first time you use one it's easy to look in the wrong place.

**What kind of work is worth calling a squad for?**
Three or more stages with different roles, or stages that depend on each other—planning, writing, design, and review in a chain. When you're personally covering several roles at once, that's exactly when to hand it to a squad.

**Will a squad just slow down simple work?**
Yes. For one-off tasks inside a single professional scope, starting a new task or finding one Work Buddy is faster and cheaper on tokens. Squads are for complex work, not a master key.

**Can I pick the squad members myself?**
You summon a ready-made squad (the Hit Content Creation Squad, say), and it decides the members. If you want a custom split, go back to the previous chapter and write out who owns what and what they hand over.
