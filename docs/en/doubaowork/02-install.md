# Download, Install, and Log In

## Download from the Official Site

Open the Doubao Work website at [doubao.com/work](https://www.doubao.com/work), click "Download Doubao Work," and pick the installer for your system and chip.

- **Mac users**: if you're not sure which chip you have, check under "About This Mac" in the Apple menu at the top-left corner;
- **Windows users**: check under Settings → System → System Information.

> **Always download from the official site only. Never get the installer from cloud drives or unknown mirrors.**

## Installation

**macOS**: open the downloaded DMG image, drag "Doubao Work" into the Applications folder, then launch it from Applications.

**Windows**: after downloading the right version for your device, double-click the installer; if Windows shows a security prompt, click "Run," follow the setup wizard, and then launch the app from the Start menu or desktop.

## Logging In

Follow the on-screen guide after first launch. You can sign in with either a **phone number** or a **Feishu account**.

> Note: the two login methods are completely separate—phone-number login and Feishu login are independent and don't share data. Pick one and stick with it, so you don't lose track of your task history.

## Updates

Click your profile picture at the bottom-left and choose "Check for Updates" from the menu. When a new version is detected, follow the prompts to update.
