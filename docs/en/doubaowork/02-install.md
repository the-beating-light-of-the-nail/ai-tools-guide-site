# How Do You Install Doubao Work? Download, Install, and Log In on Mac and Windows

You want to try Doubao Work, and more people than you'd think get stuck on step one: search for it and a dozen results come up with no clear way to tell which one is the work version; you double-click the download and nothing happens; you finally sign in and last week's tasks are gone.

This page walks you through downloading, installing, and signing in in one pass. Follow the steps and you'll be ready to use it.

## In a Hurry? These Four Lines

1. Open [doubao.com/work](https://www.doubao.com/work) and click "Download Doubao Work";
2. Once installed, launch it from Applications (Mac) or the Start menu (Windows) — don't run it from inside the installer;
3. Sign in with your phone number or your Feishu account. Pick one and don't switch later;
4. Click your profile picture at the bottom-left → "Check for Updates" to confirm you're on the latest version.

## Download from the Official Site

Open the Doubao Work website at [doubao.com/work](https://www.doubao.com/work), click "Download Doubao Work," and pick the installer for your system and chip.

- **Mac users**: if you're not sure which chip you have, check under "About This Mac" in the Apple menu at the top-left corner;
- **Windows users**: check under Settings → System → System Information.

> **Always download from the official site only.** Skip cloud-drive links, forum attachments, and anything calling itself a high-speed mirror — you have no idea what's been added to those packages.

## Installing on macOS

You'll get a DMG image. Double-click to open it, drag the "Doubao Work" icon into Applications, then launch it from Applications.

Two mistakes beginners make almost every time — learn them now:

- Running it straight from inside the DMG window. It will open, but that's a read-only temporary disk, and updates and settings tend to break. There's only one step you need: drag it into Applications, then launch it.
- Not finding the icon afterward. Search for "Doubao Work" in Launchpad, or open the Applications folder in Finder.

Once it's installed you can delete the DMG and the installer. The installed app is unaffected.

## Installing on Windows

Download the build for your device and double-click the installer. When Windows shows a security prompt, check that the publisher is the official one first, then click "Run" and follow the setup wizard. Launch it from the Start menu or the desktop.

> If your work computer blocks software installation, don't try to work around endpoint security policies. Ask your IT administrator about whitelisting or enterprise deployment.

## Logging In

Follow the on-screen guide after first launch. You can sign in with either a **phone number** or a **Feishu account**.

Read this before you choose: the two methods are isolated. A phone-number account and a Feishu account are separate — they don't share history.

So stick with whatever your company already uses. Phone number today, Feishu tomorrow, and you'll end up with half your tasks on each side. Nothing was lost — you just did half the work in two different places.

## Three Things to Do Right After Installing

1. Click your profile picture at the bottom-left → "Check for Updates" and confirm you're on the latest version;
2. Decide on your login method and don't switch midway;
3. Give it a small job to try out. Don't study the interface yet ([finish your first task in five minutes →](/en/doubaowork/04-first-task)).

## Updates

Click your profile picture at the bottom-left and choose "Check for Updates" from the menu. When a new version is detected, follow the prompts to update.

## FAQ

**The download won't open, or says it's damaged?**
Delete the installer and download it again from the official site, checking that you picked the right system and chip. If it still fails, save your system version, the installer filename, and a screenshot of the error, then go through the official feedback channel. Don't go turning off system security features.

**Nothing happens after I sign in?**
Check three things: whether your default browser is blocking the login redirect, whether a network proxy is interfering with authentication, and whether your system clock is accurate. Quit the app and reopen it. If it still fails, keep the logs and screenshots and report it.

**Can I use the Doubao app on my phone instead?**
Not the same thing. The chat app on your phone answers questions; Doubao Work delivers finished work, and it needs the desktop client. You can check progress and drive your computer remotely from your phone though — see [Remote Control Your Computer from Your Phone](/en/doubaowork/07-mobile-remote).

**Do I have to uninstall and reinstall to update?**
No. Click your profile picture at the bottom-left → "Check for Updates," then follow the prompts when a new version shows up.
