# Auto-Summarize Your Daily Report and Remind You of Tomorrow's Work

> Scenario: those few lines of the daily report are the hardest to write at the end of the day—you sat through several meetings, answered endless group messages, revised a document a few times, yet when you try to recall it, you can't say what actually moved forward.

Tomorrow's follow-ups are scattered everywhere too: some buried in chat logs, some mentioned only in passing at a meeting, some already past deadline. Let Doubao Work gather those records first, and your report and reminders start from something reliable.

Daily and weekly reports share the same process: **first collect what happened today from group messages, tasks, and the calendar → then sort it into results, decisions, to-dos, and risks → finally turn what needs follow-up into reminders**. The report draws on records made during the day—no filling it in from memory at the last minute.

## Summarizing Group Messages

Group chats mix discussion, notices, emoji reactions, and idle chatter. The more useful summary extracts **confirmed conclusions, concrete deliverables, unanswered questions, and blockers**, keeping just enough context:

```text
Please summarize today's messages in the "xxx group" and draft my daily
report.
```

The system reads the specified work group's messages from today, filters out duplicate notices and small talk, then extracts confirmed conclusions, action items, unanswered questions, and risks.

## To-Do Reminders

A reminder should help you **decide what to do first** the next day—not just read the to-do list back to you. It can rank items by deadline, dependencies, impact on others, and estimated effort:

```text
Turn today's to-dos into reminders, and at 9 tomorrow morning remind me
what to tackle first.
```

Reminders can stay inside Doubao Work; work that must start at a fixed time, or needs a blocked-out stretch, can also go into your **Feishu calendar**. The report explains what happened today; the reminders catch tomorrow's actions—both draw on the same to-do state, so tasks don't keep reappearing across different tools.

## A Steady Daily Rhythm

Check priorities in the morning, leave records in groups and tasks during the day, summarize results and risks in the evening, and carry unfinished items into the next day. The report becomes **a result of the work**, and the reminders catch the follow-through.
