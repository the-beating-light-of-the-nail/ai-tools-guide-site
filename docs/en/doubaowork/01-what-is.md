# What Is Doubao Work? A Beginner's First Lesson

You've probably already chatted with Doubao: you ask how to write a weekly report and it hands you a draft. And then? You copy, you paste, and you rewrite it into something you can actually hand in.

**Doubao Work** is an AI assistant from ByteDance built for real work tasks (entry point: [doubao.com/work](https://www.doubao.com/work)). It takes care of that "and then?" step: you say "analyze the sales data in this folder and build a reporting PPT," and it reads the files itself, runs the numbers, builds the tables, lays out the slides, and hands back a deck you can keep editing.

One line to remember the difference: chat AI gives you answers, Doubao Work hands in finished work.

## What It Actually Does for You

You're not short of work — your work is fragmented. A spreadsheet needs cleaning, pivoting, and ranking. A meeting recording needs to become minutes. One article needs three platform-specific versions. What these jobs have in common: there are materials, there's a clear deliverable, and the process is tedious.

That's exactly what Doubao Work is for. HR, education, finance, accounting, marketing, legal, research, engineering — the role doesn't matter. There's only one test: does your work include a step where someone reads a pile of materials and produces one thing?

Not sure where to begin? The [Scenario Lookup Table](/en/doubaowork/ref-scenarios) lists thirty-plus routes organized by "who you are / what you ran into," each with a ready-made prompt.

## How It Differs from an Ordinary Chat AI

Chat tools are good at giving answers; Doubao Work also reads the materials you authorize, breaks work into steps, invokes Skills and connectors, and produces files you can continue editing. Whether the result is ready to hand off is still up to you to accept.

A typical use: just tell it "analyze the sales data in this folder and create a reporting PPT," and it handles the whole process itself—reading, analyzing, building tables, and producing the deck.

## On Your First Try, Don't Ask "What Can You Do"

The most common mistake with this kind of work agent is opening with "what can you do?" The answer is usually a wall of text, and once you close it you still don't know how to use the thing.

Doubao Work works better when you start with a small deliverable:

1. **Give it materials**: hand over files, folders, or links;
2. **State the outcome**: be explicit about what deliverable you want;
3. **Set boundaries**: say what must not be changed and what to do when uncertain;
4. **Watch the process**: let it run autonomously, or confirm key steps as needed and check in anytime.

Will it make mistakes? Yes. That's why you review what it hands back: are the numbers right, did it invent any facts. It does the work, you do the acceptance check — build that split from your very first job.

On the main interface, you only need to understand three parts at first: the left side is where you find entry points, the middle shows process and results, and the bottom is where you write tasks, add materials, and control permissions (see [Main Interface, Tasks, and Projects](/en/doubaowork/03-interface)).

## FAQ

**Will it mess with files on my computer?**
Not recklessly. Every job has a permission level: Always Ask / Confirm As Needed / Allow All. For your first job, pick "Confirm As Needed" — it will check with you before touching anything important.

**Do I need to know how to write prompts?**
Just talk normally. Treat the task like briefing a colleague: where the materials are, what result you want, what must not be touched. Getting those three right beats memorizing prompt templates.

**How does it relate to the chat version of Doubao?**
Same maker, two different jobs. Chat Doubao handles "answering questions." Doubao Work handles "finishing tasks" — it reads the materials you authorize, calls Skills and connectors (a connector is the channel that lets it reach external services like Feishu docs), and outputs files you can keep editing.

**Is it a fit for my role?**
If your work ever involves "read a pile of materials, produce one thing," it's worth a try. Still unsure? Open the [Scenario Lookup Table](/en/doubaowork/ref-scenarios) and find your row by role.
