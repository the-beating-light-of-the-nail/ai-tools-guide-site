# Chapter 6: Generic Answers Not Cutting It? Put a Specialist AI Colleague on the Job

You ask WorkBuddy to analyze a sales dataset. It reads it, charts it, and summarizes the trends. Usable — but something's missing: it never asked what business goal this data serves, and it never dug into what's behind the anomalies. What you want isn't a tool that can crunch numbers; it's a veteran who knows this business. An Expert is that veteran, placed inside WorkBuddy.

## How Experts and Expert Teams Differ from Skills

WorkBuddy on its own is a general-purpose Agent that can take on any task. But general-purpose doesn't mean every domain should be handled the same way.

Given the same sales dataset, a plain Agent might read the data, generate charts, and summarize trends. A data analysis Expert first understands the business goal, then pins down the core metrics, checks data quality, hunts for anomalies, analyzes possible causes, and finally delivers actionable business recommendations.

Given the same Xiaohongshu (RED) post to write, a plain Agent focuses mostly on the content itself. A Xiaohongshu operations Expert also thinks about topic selection, titles, hook retention in the opening, product-recommendation logic, the platform's content ecosystem, and interaction design.

An Expert is defined as a role-switching mechanism: through a persona, a methodology, and a toolchain, WorkBuddy executes tasks in the identity of a domain-specific expert.

The simplest way to put it:

Plain WorkBuddy = a generalist AI colleague

Expert = an AI colleague with a clear role and professional experience

An Expert Team, meanwhile, is a collaborative execution mechanism. A team is made up of multiple Experts, with a team lead who automatically breaks down the task, assigns the work, runs it in parallel, and integrates the final delivery.

You only need to tell the team lead the client background, the latest requirements, and the expected outcome — no need to pick team members yourself or split the task manually. The lead coordinates the content, campaign, analytics, and other members, then assembles the complete proposal.

| Approach | Essence | Best for |
|-|-|-|
| Plain task | General understanding and execution | One-off tasks with clear scope |
| Skill | A specific tool capability | Repeatedly executing one action reliably |
| Expert | Persona + methodology + toolchain | Single-point professional problems in a clear domain |
| Expert Team | Multiple Experts + collaboration workflow | Complex projects needing breakdown, parallelism, and integration |

## Summoning an Expert Team

In an Expert Team, the team lead handles breakdown and integration, while members execute in parallel or in sequence by role. Before starting, confirm: whether the members' division of labor covers everything, which steps depend on previous ones, where a human decision is needed, and who does the final integration.

Open "Experts · Skills · Connectors," select "Expert Teams," and click Summon.

## FAQ

**Should I use an Expert or a Skill?**
If you have one kind of work you keep doing the same way, use a Skill — it packages the method. If you have a single-point professional problem in a clear domain and need someone who arrives with a methodology and a toolchain, use an Expert. In one line: a Skill is *how* the work gets done; an Expert is *who* does it.

**Is an Expert Team strictly better than a single Expert?**
It depends on the job. A single Expert suits one well-defined professional question; a team suits a big project that needs breaking down, running in parallel, and reassembling. You just tell the lead the background and the requirements — it divides the work and integrates the result, and you never have to pick the members yourself.

**Can I have it build me a custom Expert?**
Yes. Go to "My Experts" and create one, fill in the persona and methodology, and you can test it right after generation. It stays in "My Experts" whenever you want to call it.

**Do I need to write a long persona from scratch to summon an Expert?**
No. Pick an existing one from "Experts · Skills · Connectors," or give a rough direction at creation time and it generates one. If you want fine-grained control, tune the persona gradually from there.
