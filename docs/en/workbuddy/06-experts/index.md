# Chapter 6: WorkBuddy Experts and Expert Teams

## How Experts and Expert Teams Differ from Skills

WorkBuddy on its own is a general-purpose Agent that can take on any task. But general-purpose doesn't mean every domain should be handled the same way.



For example,

When analyzing a sales dataset, a plain Agent might read the data, generate charts, and summarize trends. A data analysis Expert first understands the business goal, then pins down the core metrics, checks data quality, hunts for anomalies, analyzes possible causes, and finally delivers actionable business recommendations.

When writing a Xiaohongshu (RED) post, a plain Agent focuses mostly on the content itself. A Xiaohongshu operations Expert also thinks about topic selection, titles, hook retention in the opening, product-recommendation logic, the platform's content ecosystem, and interaction design.



An Expert is defined as a role-switching mechanism: through a persona, a methodology, and a toolchain, WorkBuddy executes tasks in the identity of a domain-specific expert.



The simplest way to put it:

Plain WorkBuddy = a generalist AI colleague

Expert = an AI colleague with a clear role and professional experience



An Expert Team, meanwhile, is a collaborative execution mechanism. A team is made up of multiple Experts, with a team lead who automatically breaks down the task, assigns the work, runs it in parallel, and integrates the final delivery.

You only need to tell the team lead the client background, the latest requirements, and the expected outcome — no need to pick team members yourself or split the task manually. The lead coordinates the content, campaign, analytics, and other members, then assembles the complete proposal.

| Approach | Essence | Best for |
|-|-|-|
| Plain task | General understanding and execution | One-off tasks with clear scope |
| Skill | A specific tool capability | Repeatedly executing one action reliably |
| Expert | Persona + methodology + toolchain | Single-point professional problems in a clear domain |
| Expert Team | Multiple Experts + collaboration workflow | Complex projects needing breakdown, parallelism, and integration



## Summoning an Expert

1. Open "Experts · Skills · Connectors" and select "Experts";

![](/workbuddy/06-experts/assets/001_image_T8L2bOcbBo.png)



2. Click "Summon Expert"; let's use the "Gaokao, I've Got You" Expert as an example

![](/workbuddy/06-experts/assets/002_image_TlTJbNIQOo.png)

3. Provide the task, e.g. "Find me the 2026 Gaokao math exam questions"

![](/workbuddy/06-experts/assets/003_image_Hfv9bPfcIo.png)

4. Wait for the result

## Summoning an Expert Team

In an Expert Team, the team lead handles breakdown and integration, while members execute in parallel or in sequence by role. Before starting, confirm: whether the members' division of labor covers everything, which steps depend on previous ones, where a human decision is needed, and who does the final integration.



Open "Experts · Skills · Connectors," select "Expert Teams," and click Summon
